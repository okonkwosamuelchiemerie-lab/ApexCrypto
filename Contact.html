Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a deposit take?

+

Deposits typically process within 1-24 hours depending on your payment method. Bank transfers may take up to 2-3 business days.

What are the withdrawal limits?

+

You can make up to 2 withdrawals per day. Minimum withdrawal is $800 USD equivalent or 10% of your total investment, whichever is higher.

How do investments work?

+

Investments grow based on market rates which update every minute (+0.99%), plus your investment type's expected return rate. Your portfolio updates in real-time.

Is my account secure?

+

Yes, we use industry-standard encryption and security protocols to protect your personal and financial information. Your data is stored securely.

Can I change my payment method?

+

Yes, you can change your payment method for deposits and withdrawals at any time from your account settings.

What currencies do you support?

+

We support USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, AUD, CAD, CHF, CNY, INR, MXN, BRL, and NGN (Nigerian Naira).
